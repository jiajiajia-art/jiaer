# FunLoom Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 20, 2026**

## 1\. Who We Are

FunLoom is operated by Beijing Kuran Dreamon Technology LTD. In this Policy, “FunLoom,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to that operator. You can contact us at company@funloom.ai.

This Policy explains how we collect, use, retain, disclose and protect personal information when you access or use FunLoom’s websites, mobile applications, products, features, content and related services (the “Services”), and the choices available to you.

This Policy provides information about our data practices. Reading it, registering or continuing to use the Services is not blanket consent to every processing activity. Where consent or separate consent is required by law, we provide the relevant choice in the appropriate context.

## 2\. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information processed through FunLoom and the related Services. “Personal information” means information that identifies, relates to or can reasonably be linked to an individual, directly or indirectly.

Independent sign-in, payment, app store and other providers may process information under their own privacy policies. This Policy explains our collection and disclosure activities; it does not replace those providers’ disclosures about their independent processing.

## 3\. Information We Collect

We collect information according to the features you actually use, distinguishing information necessary for those features from information you choose to provide. If you do not use an optional feature, you generally do not need to provide information specific to it.

### 3.1 Accounts, Profiles and Age Information

When you register or use an account, we may process your email address, user ID, account status, sign-in method, necessary credentials or verification results, and any display name, avatar, biography, language, preferences and personalization settings you provide. We use this information for account creation and management, authentication, recovery and profile features.

If you choose third-party sign-in, we may receive authorized identifiers and profile details, such as a Google account ID, Apple account ID, WeChat OpenID or UnionID, depending on the sign-in method and permissions you select.

We process phone numbers or addresses only if you provide them or they are needed for the relevant verification, billing, support, payment, tax or compliance activity. To establish age eligibility, we may process age confirmation, an age range or necessary birth date information.

### 3.2 Uploads and Interactions

When you use creative, game, interactive, community or AI features, we may process text, prompts, messages, comments, feedback, images, photos, character artwork, videos, music, documents, game packages and other assets you submit. This supports your requests, the relevant features, storage and display, and necessary safety review.

Whether content is visible to other users depends on your publishing choices and the feature’s audience settings. Uploads may contain your own or another person’s information. Please avoid submitting sensitive information unrelated to the feature.

### 3.3 Purchases, Subscriptions and Refunds

Paid features may involve order details, products or plans, transaction identifiers, purchase history, amounts and currencies, receipts, payment and subscription status, refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, payment disputes and necessary billing information. We use these records to provide purchased access, verify transactions, handle support and disputes, and meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations.

For ordinary in-app purchases processed by an app store or third-party payment provider, we do not directly collect or store full payment card numbers.

### 3.4 Creator Verification and Payments

If you use creator monetization, payouts, rewards, redemption or similar features, we may process your legal name, necessary identity documents or verification materials, tax information, address, bank card or payout account details, and earnings, transaction, redemption and withdrawal records.

This supports verification, payments, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Relevant payment, banking or verification information may be processed by the corresponding third-party providers. Ordinary account registration does not by itself require you to submit materials specific to creator payouts.

### 3.5 Automatically Collected Device and Usage Information

We may automatically collect your IP address, device model, operating system, browser type, app version, language, mobile network information, device or anonymous identifiers, cookie identifiers and legally permitted advertising identifiers.

Usage records may include page views, clicks, shares, dwell time, session duration, feature use, likes, ratings, in-service search terms, activity participation, points, rewards, redemptions and necessary operational logs. These support service delivery, troubleshooting, security, usage analysis, experience improvements and, according to your settings, recommendations or advertising.

We may infer an approximate location from your IP address or network-level country or region information. This is not access to precise GPS location.

### 3.6 Support and Communications

When you contact us, we may process contact details, tickets, issue descriptions, feedback and supporting materials to respond, resolve problems, handle complaints or verify privacy requests.

### 3.7 Other Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Unless you voluntarily submit it, or we introduce a relevant feature with legally required notice and consent, we do not intentionally collect precise GPS location, race or ethnicity, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, address books, SMS or MMS messages, inbox emails, installed app lists, browsing history outside the Services or microphone recordings.

Files you choose to upload may contain such information. Uploading an existing audio file does not authorize us to turn on your microphone.

## 4\. Sources, Purposes and Legal Bases

Information comes from you, records generated by your use of the Services, and providers involved in the third-party sign-in, payment, verification or other features you use. The fields received depend on your authorization, settings and the feature concerned.

In addition to Section 3, we may use necessary information for account security, order, service update and administrative messages; to respond to requests; to detect and address fraud or abuse; to enforce applicable agreements; and to respond lawfully to authorized authorities. Marketing and optional advertising processing are addressed in Section 9.

Where applicable law requires us to identify a legal basis, we use the appropriate basis for each activity:

* Contractual necessity for account creation, requested functionality and order processing, to the extent necessary to perform our contract with you;
* Valid consent for sensitive preferences, optional tracking and other activities requiring consent;
* Legal obligations for tax, accounting, required verification and legally required disclosures;
* Legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and necessary service improvements where that basis is available and our interests are not overridden by your rights and interests; and
* Another legally recognized basis only where it is available for the processing concerned.

Before using information for a new purpose not described here, we provide legally required notice and obtain consent where needed. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out on the basis of consent.

## 5\. Sensitive Profile Preferences

FunLoom may allow you to voluntarily provide or select sensitive preferences, such as sexual orientation, for personalization, content recommendations and related in-app notifications. This information is not necessary for basic features; choosing not to provide it may make some recommendations less relevant.

We process it only where permitted by law and obtain explicit or separate consent where required. You may update or remove preferences or withdraw consent in available in-app settings, or request this through our contact email.

After a removal or withdrawal request, we stop using the information for future personalization within a reasonable processing period, subject to necessary retention required or permitted by law.

We do not use sensitive preferences such as sexual orientation for third-party ad targeting unless we provide additional notice and obtain legally required consent. Voluntary submission does not release us from applicable protection obligations.

## 6\. Information Used in AI Features

When you use AI-assisted, chat, image, audio, video, game or content-generation features, we and relevant AI, content-processing or moderation providers may process your input, necessary context, uploads and generated results to produce responses, provide features, review safety, detect abuse, troubleshoot and maintain service quality.

For features powered by external models, information needed to complete a request may be sent to the relevant model provider. Our disclosures and provider processing must have an appropriate legal basis and comply with applicable data protection and security requirements.

**We do not use your personal information, inputs, uploaded materials, AI-generated results or feedback to train or fine-tune AI models. We also do not permit third-party model providers serving us to use that information or content to train or fine-tune their AI models.**

We may still process the relevant information, and retain it for necessary periods under this Policy, to generate responses, review safety, troubleshoot and meet legal obligations. Not using information for training does not mean that no such necessary processing or record retention occurs. We do not repurpose this information for model training or fine-tuning under a general service-improvement purpose.

Avoid providing unnecessary identity, medical, financial, confidential or other people’s sensitive information to AI features. Retention, disclosure and requests concerning that information are addressed in Sections 10–14.

## 8\. Cookies, SDKs and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, software development kits (SDKs), pixels, analytics and attribution tools to maintain sign-in, remember preferences, store anonymous identifiers, operate features, analyze performance, prevent fraud and support advertising.

Providers may include sign-in, payment, cloud, security, analytics, advertising, attribution or content-processing services. Depending on the feature and settings, these technologies may process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, technical logs, session duration and app or advertising interactions.

Necessary sign-in and security technologies are distinguished from technologies used for optional analytics, advertising or other non-essential purposes. Where prior consent is required, we enable the relevant non-essential technologies only after consent.

You may manage cookies, advertising identifiers and related permissions through available browser, device, platform or in-app settings. You can also contact our email address for information or applicable opt-out requests. Declining optional tracking does not affect basic features that do not depend on it.

## 9\. Advertising, Recommendations and Marketing Choices

FunLoom may display third-party advertising. We and advertising partners may process legally permitted device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, app interactions, impressions, clicks, conversions and attribution data to deliver, measure and improve ads and prevent fraud.

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. If actual advertising, analytics or attribution activities qualify as a “sale,” “sharing” or targeted advertising under applicable law, you may exercise the corresponding opt-out rights. We honor applicable opt-out signals where legally required, and you can submit requests through our contact email.

You may limit relevant processing through device or browser advertising settings and any in-app privacy choices available. Closing a single advertisement does not opt you out of all ads, withdraw tracking consent or disable personalization.

For marketing emails, use the unsubscribe option provided or contact us. Necessary account security, transaction and service messages may still be sent. You can manage device notifications in system settings.

We may recommend content based on your selected preferences and interactions. Use available settings to adjust preferences or contact us with personalization requests. For automated decisions that legally qualify as having legal or similarly significant effects, applicable rights to explanations, objections or human review are respected. This does not mean every ordinary content recommendation is such a decision.

## 11\. Retention, Deletion and Backups

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Retention criteria include:

* Accounts and profiles: the period needed to provide account features and handle changes or deletion requests;
* Content, conversations and uploads: the period needed for requested content features, saved records and related deletion requests;
* Sensitive preferences: the period needed for selected personalization, with that use ending after removal or withdrawal;
* Device and security logs: the period needed for troubleshooting, fraud prevention, security investigations and necessary disputes;
* Transactions, payouts, verification and tax records: the period needed for payments, refunds, accounting, tax, compliance and statutory recordkeeping; and
* Support records: the period needed to respond, resolve issues and perform necessary follow-up checks.

We consider the information’s nature, purpose, account status, your choices, actual disputes and applicable legal requirements. Once the purpose is fulfilled and there is no lawful need to retain information, we delete or anonymize it. Possible future usefulness alone does not justify indefinite retention of identifiable data.

Account or content deletion does not necessarily result in immediate deletion across all systems. Backups are handled according to the applicable backup and clearing cycles. Until clearing is complete, access to and use of information no longer needed for normal operations are restricted. Tax, security or dispute records retained by law must not be reused for unrelated purposes.

Anonymized or aggregated information that cannot reasonably identify an individual may be used lawfully for statistics, research, security and product improvement. Merely replacing a name or user ID does not necessarily anonymize information.

## 14\. Account Deletion and Subscriptions

You can request deletion of your FunLoom account in the app:

1. Open FunLoom.
2. Go to Personal Center.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
4. Select Delete Account.
5. Follow the prompts to confirm.

If you cannot use this option, contact company@funloom.ai. We process requests after necessary verification and explain any information that cannot be deleted immediately and why, as required by law.

The account will be closed or deactivated following deletion. Associated information is deleted, anonymized or retained only where lawfully necessary, as described in Section 11. Deleting particular information, withdrawing a particular consent and closing the whole account are separate requests that you may make independently.

Deleting a FunLoom account does not necessarily cancel subscriptions managed by Google Play, Apple App Store or another third party. Manage or cancel unwanted subscriptions through the relevant platform; account deletion is not a substitute.
