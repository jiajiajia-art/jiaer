13. Account Deletion

You may delete your FunLoom account directly in the app.

To delete your account:

  1. Open FunLoom.
  1. Go to Personal Center.
  1. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
  1. Tap Delete Account.
  1. Confirm your deletion request.

After you confirm account deletion, your FunLoom account will be closed or deactivated, and you will no longer be able to access the account or use account-based features.

Account deletion is irreversible. After deletion, you may lose access to your account, content, saved preferences, activity records, rewards, redemptions, purchases, subscriptions, or other account-related features.

Deleting your account does not necessarily cancel subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid services managed by Google Play, Apple App Store, or another third-party payment platform. You may need to manage or cancel those subscriptions through the applicable platform.

After account deletion, we may retain certain information where necessary or permitted for legitimate business, legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, payment, tax, accounting, compliance, backup, or enforcement purposes. Examples may include payment records, transaction records, security logs, fraud prevention records, compliance records, and backup copies retained for a limited period.

If you would like to request deletion of personal information associated with your account, you may contact us using the contact information below. We will process deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and subject to identity verification, technical feasibility, legal obligations, and permitted retention exceptions.
